How it works, and how it checks out

Demand is 19,954 cells of 100 m built from Meta's 30 m population layer, scaled to the notebook's 2026 totals, with Census 2020 shares aged 65+. Each step adds the hexagon centre that helps most, keeping every existing facility. Travel time is straight-line distance times a street-detour factor fitted to the notebook's routed results. {{ checkLine }}
